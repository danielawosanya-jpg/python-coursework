Straight answers

Will someone call me?

No. We ask for an email, not a phone number, and we don't buy or look up phone numbers. If you want to talk, there's a booking link in the report.

Is this a quote?

No. It's an educational estimate of where your protection is thin. It is not an offer of coverage, a rate, or a guarantee that any claim would be paid. Nothing here is insurance, legal or tax advice.

How accurate is the math?

It uses standard industry methods — DIME for life needs, income multiples for disability, replacement-cost ratios for property. Those are the same rules of thumb behind a paid needs analysis. They're a starting point, not a substitute for reading your actual policy.

What do you do with my answers?

They generate your report and stay in our own database. We don't sell, rent or share lead data with third parties, and unsubscribing stops every email immediately.

I already have an agent.

Good. Run the report and bring it to them. A second opinion that costs nothing is the cheapest due diligence in personal finance.
